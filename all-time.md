# All-Time Novels

## Tier 1
*Most revolutionary use of discovering a consciousness through time.*

1. **An Artist of the Floating World** (Ishiguro, 1986) 
2. **The Remains of the Day** (Ishiguro, 1989) 
3. **Sátántangó** (Krasznahorkai, 1985)
4. **The Melancholy of Resistance** (Krasznahorkai, 1989)

## Tier 2

5. **The Rings of Saturn** (Sebald, 1995) 
6. **Austerlitz** (Sebald, 2001) 
7. **The Hunting Gun** (Inoue, 1949) 
8. **Never Let Me Go** (Ishiguro, 2005)
9.  **Klara and the Sun** (Ishiguro, 2021) 

## Tier 3
*Radically inventive but not in the manner above.*

10. **The Trial** (Kafka, 1925) 
11. **The Stranger** (Camus, 1942)

15. **1984** (Orwell, 1949)
16. **Grapes of Wrath** (Steinbeck, 1939)
17. **Frankenstein** (Shelley, 1818)
18. **The Catcher in the Rye** (Salinger, 1951)
19. **On the Road** (Kerouac, 1957)
