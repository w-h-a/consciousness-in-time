# All-Time Novels

## Tier 1
*Most revolutionary use of discovering a consciousness through time.*

1. **In Search of Lost Time** (Proust, 1913-27)
2. **An Artist of the Floating World** (Ishiguro, 1986) 
3. **The Remains of the Day** (Ishiguro, 1989) 
4. **The Rings of Saturn** (Sebald, 1995) 
5. **Austerlitz** (Sebald, 2001) 

## Tier 2
*Refines the above without founding it.*

6. **Satantango** (Krasznahorkai, 1985)
7. **The Melancholy of Resistance** (Krasznahorkai, 1989) 
8. **The Hunting Gun** (Inoue, 1949) 
9. **Never Let Me Go** (Ishiguro, 2005)
10. **Klara and the Sun** (Ishiguro, 2021) 

## Tier 3
*Radical invention in the novel but not in the manner above.*

11. **His Master's Voice** (Lem, 1968) 
12. **The Trial** (Kafka, 1925) 
13. **Madame Bovary** (Flaubert, 1857) 
14. **The Metamorphosis** (Kafka, 1915)
15. **The Stranger** (Camus, 1942)

## Tier 4
*Constructed mastery. Monumental, superbly engineered, perfecting the form rather than inventing a use of it.*

16. **The Brothers Karamazov** (Dostoevsky, 1880)
17. **Crime and Punishment** (Dostoevsky, 1866)
18. **Great Expectations** (Dickens, 1860–61)
19. **The Plague** (Camus, 1947) 
20. **Adventures of Huckleberry Finn** (Twain, 1884)
21. **Nineteen Eighty-Four** (Orwell, 1949)
22. **The Grapes of Wrath** (Steinbeck, 1939)
23. **Frankenstein** (Shelley, 1818)
24. **The Catcher in the Rye** (Salinger, 1951)
25. **On the Road** (Kerouac, 1957)
